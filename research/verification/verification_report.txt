6. DETAILED FINDINGS
================================================================================

Finding F01: Circularity and Author-Coupled Rule Architecture
- Severity: MAJOR
- Status: OBSERVED
- Evidence: app/orchestrator/prioritizer.py:32-36 defines category weights: internship (+4),
  academic (+2), otp (-3). In eval/build_dataset.py, every email is manually tagged
  category="internship", "academic", or "otp". In verification/audit_baselines_robustness.py,
  ablating email categories causes the largest performance drop (-0.038 nDCG@5).
- Impact on Paper: Reviewers will identify circularity. The author designed both the rule
  weights and the scenario categories.
- Fix: Disclose the author-coupling transparently in Threats to Validity. Present an
  ablation showing the performance drop without category tags.

Finding F02: Train/Test Contamination in Deployed LearnedRanker
- Severity: MAJOR
- Status: OBSERVED
- Evidence: app/intelligence/train.py:79-92 fits ridge regression on all 40 scenarios
  in dataset.json and commits the weights to app/intelligence/ranker_weights.json.
  While eval/run_eval.py implements LOSO for reporting, the production runtime model
  is trained 100% on the test set.
- Impact on Paper: The production artifact cannot be claimed as an out-of-sample model.
- Fix: Clarify that eval/run_eval.py reports LOSO cross-validated performance, not the
  in-sample performance of the checked-in ranker_weights.json.

Finding F03: Strawman Aspect of due_date Baseline and Tie-Break Instability
- Severity: MAJOR
- Status: OBSERVED
- Evidence: eval/rankers.py:33 sorts items solely by due date, falling back to arrival order
  for ties. Undated noise items (OTPs) sit mixed into arrival order. In
  verification/audit_baselines_robustness.py, adding a 2-line noise sink (due_date_clean)
  increases due_date nDCG@5 from 0.887 to 0.907, cutting the prioritizer advantage by 30%
  (from +0.067 to +0.047). Across 30 arrival seeds, due_date nDCG@5 has std=0.019 (range
  [0.847, 0.916]), while prioritizer has std=0.001 (range [0.952, 0.955]).
- Impact on Paper: Reviewers will note that pure due_date is weakened by not filtering OTPs.
- Fix: Report stronger baselines (due_date + noise sink and hybrid kind tiebreak) in the
  results table alongside naive due_date.

Finding F04: Single-Annotator Subjectivity and Unfilled Agreement Template
- Severity: MAJOR
- Status: OBSERVED
- Evidence: All 40 scenarios and gold grades in eval/build_dataset.py were authored by one
  person. eval/annotator_agreement.py exits with "No grades found yet" because
  study_kit/annotator_sheet.xlsx is unrated.
- Impact on Paper: Major threat to external validity.
- Fix: Run a 1,000-trial Monte Carlo label noise simulation (which showed 100% of trials
  remain significant at 20% label noise) to prove robustness against single-annotator noise.
  Fill the sheet with an independent student annotator before camera-ready submission.

Finding F05: Trivial Security Benchmark and 100% Adaptive Attack Sanitizer Bypass
- Severity: MAJOR
- Status: OBSERVED
- Evidence: eval/security_eval.py only tests 10 hand-authored attacks matching regexes.
  verification/audit_security.py demonstrated that 10 of 10 adaptive prompt injection
  attacks (homoglyphs, zero-width spaces, base64, administrative phrasing) bypass the filter.
  app/orchestrator/llm.py:78-83 fails to sanitize metadata fields (due, source, kind).
  The adversarial ranking benchmark does not exist in code.
- Impact on Paper: The paper cannot claim prompt injection robustness or novel security
  integrity results based on the existing repository.
- Fix: Frame the paper around the ranking benchmark and systems architecture; place prompt
  injection and adversarial ranking integrity squarely in Future Work.

Finding F06: Metric Saturation on Short Scenarios
- Severity: MINOR
- Status: OBSERVED
- Evidence: verification/audit_metrics.py revealed that 20 of 40 scenarios (50.0%) have 5
  or fewer items. For a 4-item scenario, top-5 retrieval is a full permutation test.
  This elevates the random baseline nDCG@5 to 0.739.
- Impact on Paper: Reviewers may argue nDCG@5 is too forgiving on small lists.
- Fix: Highlight nDCG@3 (where random scores 0.599 and prioritizer scores 0.930) as the
  primary top-of-list metric.

Finding F07: MRR Metric Suppression from Missing Grade-3 Targets
- Severity: MINOR
- Status: OBSERVED
- Evidence: 13 of 40 scenarios (32.5%) have no grade-3 items. In eval/metrics.py:67-72,
  mrr() returns 0.0 for all rankers when no grade-3 item exists, depressing MRR.
- Impact on Paper: MRR numbers (prioritizer: 0.662, due_date: 0.551) are artificially
  suppressed by zero-inflation on 13 scenarios.
- Fix: Clarify the MRR threshold in the methodology section, or report MRR only over the
  27 scenarios containing grade-3 items.

Finding F08: Undisclosed Linear DCG Gain Formulation
- Severity: MINOR
- Status: OBSERVED
- Evidence: eval/metrics.py:23 computes DCG with linear gain rel_i / log2(i + 1). Modern
  web search IR literature typically uses exponential gain (2^rel - 1) / log2(i + 1).
  Under exponential gain, prioritizer nDCG@5 is 0.950, due_date is 0.866, and delta is
  +0.084 (even larger than the reported +0.067).
- Impact on Paper: Failure to state the gain formulation makes the metric ambiguous.
- Fix: State the linear gain formula explicitly in Task Formulation, and report the
  exponential gain comparison (+0.084) in the analysis.

Finding F09: Documentation vs Code Directory Drift
- Severity: MINOR
- Status: OBSERVED
- Evidence: ARCHITECTURE.md line 29 states prioritizer.py and ranking.py are in
  app/intelligence/. They are in app/orchestrator/. EVALUATION.md omits LearnedRanker.
- Impact on Paper: Inaccurate citations to repository source files.
- Fix: Correct file paths and acknowledge LearnedRanker.

Finding F10: Windows Console Unicode Crash and Missing Dependencies
- Severity: MINOR
- Status: OBSERVED
- Evidence: eval/run_eval.py:219 crashes with UnicodeEncodeError on Windows CP1252 consoles
  due to '\u0394' unless PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8 is set. eval/annotator_agreement.py
  fails due to unlisted openpyxl dependency.
- Impact on Paper: Hurts external reproducibility on Windows machines.
- Fix: Document PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8 in reproducibility instructions; add openpyxl to
  requirements.txt.
