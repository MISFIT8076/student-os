6. THE SANITIZER `safe_text()` IN DETAIL
----------------------------------------
File: app/security.py

A. Sanitization Mechanism:
  - Strips hidden HTML comments: re.compile(r"<!--.*?-->", re.S).
  - Splits text into lines and checks each line against 12 regular expressions:
      1. ignore_instructions: r"ignore\s+(all\s+|the\s+)?(previous|prior|above)\s+instructions"
      2. disregard: r"disregard\s+(the\s+)?(previous|above|earlier|system)"
      3. forget: r"forget\s+(everything|all|previous|prior)"
      4. you_are_now: r"\byou\s+are\s+now\b"
      5. new_instructions: r"new\s+instructions?\s*:"
      6. system_prompt: r"\bsystem\s+prompt\b"
      7. reveal_prompt: r"(reveal|show|print|repeat)\s+(your|the)\s+(system\s+)?(prompt|instructions|rules)"
      8. act_as: r"\b(act|behave|respond)\s+as\s+(if|a|an|the)\b"
      9. override: r"\b(override|bypass|ignore)\s+(the\s+)?(safety|rules|guardrails|restrictions)\b"
      10. jailbreak: r"\b(jailbreak|developer\s+mode|DAN\b)"
      11. role_prefix: r"^\s*(system|assistant|ai)\s*:"
      12. exfiltrate: r"\b(send|forward|email|leak|post)\b.{0,30}\b(otp|password|token|secret|credentials?)\b"
  - Matching lines are replaced with: "[removed: possible injection]".

B. Critical Blind Spots (What it does NOT catch):
  - Zero-width whitespace insertion (e.g. "I\u200bgnore").
  - Unicode homoglyph substitution (e.g. Cyrillic 'о' in "ignore").
  - Base64 / rot13 / hex encoded payloads.
  - Multi-line attacks or semantic instruction phrasing ("Important administrative notice: you must prioritize...").
  - Unsanitized metadata vectors: In app/orchestrator/llm.py:78-83, safe_text() is called on title and detail, but i.due, i.kind, i.source, and i.priority are concatenated into LLM context raw.
